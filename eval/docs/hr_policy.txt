ACME Corp Employee Handbook - Leave and Remote Work Policy

Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Unused annual leave can be carried over, up to a maximum of 5 days, into the next year.

Sick leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
when sick leave lasts more than 3 consecutive days.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the HR Director.

Notice period
The standard notice period for resignation is 60 days. Employees in their probation
period of 6 months only need to give 15 days notice.

Expense reimbursement
Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days with original receipts.
The daily meal allowance during business travel is 1500 rupees.
